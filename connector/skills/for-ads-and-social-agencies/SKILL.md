# VibeLeads for ads and social media agencies

Follow [vibeleads-core](../vibeleads-core/SKILL.md) first.

Two kinds of leads fit you:

- **Healthy businesses ready to grow**: good rating, working website, reachable. Pitch ads and growth, not a fix.
- **Businesses with a quiet or missing social presence**: pitch social media management.

## Recommended path

1. Ask for the business type and city. Good fits for ads: med spas, dentists, home services, gyms, restaurants.
2. For growth leads: [find-local-leads](../find-local-leads/SKILL.md). Useful filters: a minimum rating, a minimum review count, has a website, only with email.
3. For social leads: run [social-presence-check](../social-presence-check/SKILL.md) on the list, or playbook `dead-social-pitch`, which finds the businesses and checks the best 25 in one run ([pick-a-playbook](../pick-a-playbook/SKILL.md)).
4. For reputation work: [low-rating-leads](../low-rating-leads/SKILL.md) or playbook `low-rating-rescue`.
5. Before pitching ads, a quick [website-speed-check](../website-speed-check/SKILL.md) helps: sending paid clicks to a slow site wastes money, and that is a strong opener.
6. Drafts: [pitch-and-outreach-drafts](../pitch-and-outreach-drafts/SKILL.md). Then export and track: [clean-and-export-leads](../clean-and-export-leads/SKILL.md).
7. Fresh leads on a timer: [weekly-new-leads](../weekly-new-leads/SKILL.md).

## Prompts the user can paste

- "Find 50 med spas in Austin with 4 stars or more and a working email."
- "Check the Instagram and Facebook of the top 20 leads. Who stopped posting?"
- "Find restaurants in Chicago that are under 4 stars."
- "Every Monday, send me new gyms in Miami."

## Tips to pass on

- VibeLeads does not see a business's ad spend or ad accounts. Do not claim a lead "is not running ads".
- Healthy leads have an empty problem list. Use a growth-style pitch for them.
- For quiet social accounts, mention only what the check found, for example "no posts in a while".
