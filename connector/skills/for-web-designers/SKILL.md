# VibeLeads for web designers

Follow [vibeleads-core](../vibeleads-core/SKILL.md) first.

Your best leads are local businesses with no website, or a slow, broken or not secure one.

## Recommended path

1. Ask for the business type and city they want to target. If unsure, suggest trades that depend on local search: dentists, roofers, salons, plumbers, restaurants.
2. Pick one:
   - No website at all → [no-website-or-slow-site-leads](../no-website-or-slow-site-leads/SKILL.md) with the no-website filter, or playbook `no-website-finder`.
   - Has a site that needs a redesign → playbook `website-redesign-pitch`, or a normal search then [website-speed-check](../website-speed-check/SKILL.md).
3. Narrow to the ones worth calling: [hot-leads-only](../hot-leads-only/SKILL.md).
4. Write the first messages: [pitch-and-outreach-drafts](../pitch-and-outreach-drafts/SKILL.md).
5. Export and track: [clean-and-export-leads](../clean-and-export-leads/SKILL.md).
6. Keep it flowing: [weekly-new-leads](../weekly-new-leads/SKILL.md).

Check which playbooks are live with [pick-a-playbook](../pick-a-playbook/SKILL.md) before naming one to the user. Listing them with the service "web design" shows the ones that fit.

Also useful: playbook `new-business-finder` (new businesses often need a first website).

## Prompts the user can paste

- "Find 40 dentists in Austin, TX with no website."
- "Find 50 roofers in Dallas and check the speed of the top 20 sites."
- "Show me only the hot leads from that list and write a short email for the top 5."
- "Every week, find new salons in London with no website."

## Tips to pass on

- No-website leads with many reviews are the easiest win: busy business, nothing online.
- For slow sites, quote the measured load time in the pitch.
- A lead with a phone number but no email is still good. Offer a call script.
