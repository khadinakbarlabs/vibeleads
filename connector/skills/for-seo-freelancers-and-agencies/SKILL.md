# VibeLeads for SEO freelancers and agencies

Follow [vibeleads-core](../vibeleads-core/SKILL.md) first.

Your best leads are established local businesses with a website that has clear search problems.

## Recommended path

1. Ask for the business type and city.
2. Build a list of businesses that have a website: [find-local-leads](../find-local-leads/SKILL.md) with the has-website filter. Or use playbook `weak-seo-pitch`, which does steps 2 and 3 in one run ([pick-a-playbook](../pick-a-playbook/SKILL.md)).
3. Audit the top 10 sites: [seo-audit](../seo-audit/SKILL.md). Add the broken links check for a stronger pitch.
4. Optional extras that sell well alongside SEO:
   - [website-speed-check](../website-speed-check/SKILL.md): speed affects search.
   - [ai-visibility-check](../ai-visibility-check/SKILL.md): shows who an AI assistant names for "best (type) in (city)". One sample answer per city.
   - [low-rating-leads](../low-rating-leads/SKILL.md): reviews matter for local search.
5. Write outreach from the audit findings: [pitch-and-outreach-drafts](../pitch-and-outreach-drafts/SKILL.md).
6. Export and track replies: [clean-and-export-leads](../clean-and-export-leads/SKILL.md).

## Prompts the user can paste

- "Find 30 law firms in Denver that have a website, then do the SEO audit on the top 10."
- "Check for weak SEO on my saved list."
- "Which of these sites have broken links?"
- "Write a short email for each audited site using the problems you found."

## Tips to pass on

- The SEO audit covers 10 sites per run. Run it on the best-scored leads first.
- Pitch the exact findings. Never promise a ranking position.
- After 10 marked leads, check which problem gets the most replies and lead with that.
